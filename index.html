N.003The fine printN.003
  1. Proviso 1, More work: about 20 hours of work a week rose to about 30. Agta who farmed more had about ten fewer hours of leisure a week. Source: Dyble et al., 2019.
  2. Proviso 2, Smaller bodies: average men fell from 175 cm to 160 cm and women from 165 cm to 152 cm by 3000 BC. Source: Jared Diamond, 1987.
  3. Proviso 3, Crowd diseases: measles, smallpox and flu reached people through livestock and crowded settlements; a band of 30 on the move gives them nowhere to spread. Source: Jared Diamond, 1997.
  4. Proviso 4, Three grains: of about 50,000 edible plants, 15 provide 90% of our calories and rice, wheat and maize two thirds of that. Source: FAO, 1995.
  5. Proviso 5, Someone is king: stored grain created guards, counters and rulers; the first named person in history, Kushim, was a barley accountant. Source: Yuval Noah Harari, 2011.
  6. Proviso 6, No way back: world population grew from about 5 million in 10,000 BC to 8.2 billion today. Source: UN.
